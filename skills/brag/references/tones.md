# Tone reference — Arabic

Seven tones. Each changes scripting energy, pacing, typography personality, and transition style. All example copy is in Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) by default — when a dialect is specified, adapt the examples to that dialect while preserving the tone's energy.

---

## `default`

**Energy:** Playful, clean, postable. The product gets to be funny on its own terms.

**Voice:** First person plural. Warm. Direct. No corporate language.

**Typography:** Mixed weight. Comfortable. Let the Arabic letterforms breathe — generous line-height.

**Pacing:** 4-5 scenes. Each scene 3-5 seconds. Comfortable rhythm.

**Hook style:** A simple question or observation that sets up the reveal.
```
تطبيقات المواعدة صُممت للبشر.
خطأ واضح.
```
*(Dating apps were designed for humans. Obvious mistake.)*

**Highlight style:** Short punchy phrases. One idea per scene.
```
تصفّح الأحصنة المؤهلة بالقرب من إسطبلك.
```
*(Browse eligible horses near your stable.)*

**Outro style:** The product name, then a tagline. Light punchline.
```
تندر للخيول.
اعثر على شريكك المثالي في الإسطبل.
```
*(Horse Tinder. Find your perfect stablemate.)*

**Transitions:** Crossfade or clean slide.

**When to use:** Most absurd consumer apps. Projects that have personality without trying too hard.

---

## `polished`

**Energy:** Serious, elegant. Uses restraint as the creative choice.

**Voice:** Third person or no voice. The product speaks for itself.

**Typography:** Mixed case. Light-to-medium weight. Generous spacing. Nothing aggressive. Arabic Naskh-style fonts work beautifully here.

**Pacing:** 3-4 scenes. Each scene 4-6 seconds. Confidence through slow reveals.

**Hook style:** A single strong image or the product name at full scale.

**Highlight style:** One feature per scene. No bullets. No lists.
```
شهادة معتمدة
عند الإتمام.
```
*(Certified credential upon completion.)*

**Outro style:** Product name. Tagline. Silence.

**Transitions:** Slow crossfade (0.6-0.8s).

**When to use:** Projects that aren't jokes. Products that want to feel premium. Anytime the user says "clean" or "elegant."

---

## `yc-parody`

**Energy:** Deadpan startup launch energy. The joke is how seriously it's delivered.

**Voice:** Serious. Matter-of-fact. No winking. The absurdity comes from the product, not the tone.

**Typography:** Sentence case. Heavy weight or medium weight. Monospace for data points. No decoration.

**Pacing:** 4-5 scenes. Structured. Each scene makes one claim.

**Hook style:** The problem, stated completely seriously.
```
كل يوم، سيارات الأجرة تنقلنا.
لكن مَن ينقل سيارات الأجرة؟
```
*(Every day, taxis carry us. But who carries the taxis?)*

**Highlight style:** Feature or metric stated as fact.
```
متوفر في ١٢ مدينة.
نسبة تشغيل الأسطول ٩٩٫١٪
```
*(Available in 12 cities. 99.1% fleet uptime.)*

**Outro style:** Product name. The tagline. A URL that implies legitimacy.
```
تاكسي للتاكسيات
تطبيق نقل الركاب لأصول نقل الركاب.
taxifortaxis.com
```
*(Taxi for Taxis. The ride-hailing app for ride-hailing assets.)*

**Transitions:** Hard cut or minimal crossfade (0.2s).

**When to use:** Any recursive or absurd concept that benefits from being played straight.

---

## `chaotic`

**Energy:** Fast, loud, unhinged. The video is the joke.

**Voice:** Aggressive. SHORT WORDS. Metric dumps. Confidence is louder than exclamation marks.

**Typography:** UPPERCASE for emphasis (use Arabic emphasis: bold, larger size, color — Arabic doesn't have uppercase). Overlapping layers. Intentional mess.

**Pacing:** 6-8 scenes. Some under 2 seconds. Rapid cuts.

**Hook style:** A number, claim, or challenge that demands attention.
```
٣٫٥ مليون حمامة
تم تتبعها.
```
*(3.5 million pigeons tracked.)*

**Highlight style:** Fast stat dumps. Overlapping info. Controlled chaos.
```
GPS | حرارة | رطوبة | حَمَامَة مُكتَشَفَة
```
*(GPS | temperature | humidity | pigeon detected)*

**Outro style:** The product name, big. Maybe repeated. A URL thrown in.
```
حمامة.ai
حمامة.ai
حمامة.ai
```

**Transitions:** Hard cut. Glitch. Scale shift.

**When to use:** When the product is so ridiculous that restraint would be dishonest.

---

## `deadpan`

**Energy:** Still. Quiet. The humor is in the emptiness.

**Voice:** Minimal. One line at a time. Long pauses.

**Typography:** Light weight. Big size. Lots of negative space. Arabic calligraphic fonts can amplify the effect.

**Pacing:** 3-4 scenes. Each scene 5-7 seconds. Silence is the transition.

**Hook style:** One word. Or one short phrase. Then nothing.
```
حاسبة.
```
*(Calculator.)*

**Highlight style:** One feature, stated flatly.
```
تجمع.
```
*(It adds.)*

**Outro style:** The product. Nothing else.
```
حاسبة.
تفعل ما تتوقعه بالضبط.
```
*(Calculator. Does exactly what you'd expect.)*

**Transitions:** Cut to black. Hold. New scene.

**When to use:** When the product is deliberately simple or the contrast between simplicity and the video format is the joke.

---

## `cinematic`

**Energy:** Grand. Dramatic. Big feelings for a small product.

**Voice:** Third person. Narrator energy. Sweeping statements.

**Typography:** Heavy weight display type. Full-bleed frames. Arabic Kufi-style fonts at massive scale.

**Pacing:** 4-5 scenes. Each 4-6 seconds. Big reveals.

**Hook style:** An establishing shot. A universal statement.
```
في عالمٍ مليءٍ بالقوائم...
واحدة فقط تفهمك.
```
*(In a world full of to-do lists... only one understands you.)*

**Highlight style:** Feature as emotional beat.
```
كل مهمة.
كل إنجاز.
كل لحظة تُحسب.
```
*(Every task. Every achievement. Every moment counts.)*

**Outro style:** The product. The tagline. Hold.
```
مهامّ.
أنجز أكثر. فكّر أقل.
```
*(Tasks. Do more. Think less.)*

**Transitions:** Slow crossfade. Dramatic wipe. Scale up.

**When to use:** When the product deserves (or hilariously doesn't deserve) epic treatment.

---

## `app-store`

**Energy:** Clean, professional, trustworthy. Feature-forward. Like a real app store preview.

**Voice:** Second person. Direct. "Your X, simplified."

**Typography:** Clean sans-serif. Medium weight. Centered or right-aligned. Professional Arabic — IBM Plex Sans Arabic shines here.

**Pacing:** 4-6 scenes. Each 3-5 seconds. Feature cards.

**Hook style:** The problem or the promise, cleanly stated.
```
إدارة فريقك.
بطريقة أبسط.
```
*(Manage your team. The simpler way.)*

**Highlight style:** One feature per card. Icon + label + one-liner.
```
📊 لوحة تحكم فورية
شاهد كل شيء في لمحة واحدة.
```
*(📊 Instant dashboard. See everything at a glance.)*

**Outro style:** Product logo. Call to action. Download prompt or URL.
```
تيم فلو
ابدأ مجاناً اليوم.
teamflow.app
```
*(TeamFlow. Start free today.)*

**Transitions:** Clean slide or card flip. No mess.

**When to use:** When the product is real, the audience is professional, or the user wants something they could actually post as an ad.
